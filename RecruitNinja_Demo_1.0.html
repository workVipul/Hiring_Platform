Product overview

Three capabilities, one workflow.

MODULE 01
JD Generation
Turns a brief, a call, or a chat into a complete, on-brand job description in minutes.
MODULE 02
Candidate Sourcing
Scores and ranks every candidate in Zoho Recruit against the published JD.
MODULE 03
Admin & SLA Control
Keeps templates, formats and turnaround times consistent and configurable.

All three run on top of Zoho Recruit, our existing system of record, rather than replacing it.
